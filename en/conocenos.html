Where each number comes from

Every number says where it came from.

Beside every figure you will see a bar of five marks. Five marks: the document exists and can be opened. One mark: someone said it and nobody checked it. And if a figure is missing to build a total, we do not give you the total: we tell you what is missing.

  1. There is a document

    5 of 5 · Verified

    The invoice, the bill of lading or the customs declaration. The paper is there and it can be opened.

  2. We checked it ourselves

    4 of 5 · Measured

    There is no paper from the supplier, but we went to the source and contrasted it. We say against what.

  3. It is a calculation

    3 of 5 · Estimated

    It is not a number picked at random: it comes with the arithmetic in plain sight for you to check.

  4. The supplier says so

    2 of 5 · Self-reported

    They passed it to us and we have not checked it yet. It is useful to get your bearings, not to sign.

  5. No backing

    1 of 5 · Unverified

    It came in with nothing behind it. It is marked as such and is not used to decide anything.
