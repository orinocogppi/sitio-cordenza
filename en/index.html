01 / The moment

Venezuela is at a decisive moment.

Its business and industrial activity needs a modern infrastructure to coordinate requests, supply, information and execution with greater speed, confidence and clarity.

  1. 01

    Processes off the record

    Requests, quotes, documents and decisions move between files, emails, calls and conversations that never share a complete picture.

  2. 02

    Information that is hard to compare

    Every supplier presents a different scope, currency, terms, lead time and cost. Price on its own rarely represents the whole operation.

  3. 03

    Commercial capacity scattered

    There are buyers, manufacturers, distributors, contractors and operators with real capacity, but finding and coordinating them takes relationships and market knowledge.

  4. 04

    Execution that demands follow-through

    Checking specifications, negotiating documentation and coordinating contracting requires constant commercial and operational attention.

We come from trade. We know first-hand how hard it can be to find dependable supply, negotiate competitive terms and coordinate a delivery. Cordenza exists to turn that complexity into an organised operation.

A single coordination system

From the need to an operation ready to execute.

Cordenza receives requests of any level of complexity, activates the market, structures the offers and coordinates each process until it becomes a purchase, sale or contract ready to move forward.

  1. 01

    We understand your need

    We receive descriptions, lists, files, photographs, references or technical specifications. We organise products, quantities, terms and validation criteria.

  2. 02

    We find applicable supply

    We activate manufacturers, distributors, suppliers, contractors and partners with the capacity to answer the request.

  3. 03

    We compare the alternatives

    We contrast specifications, documentation, quantities, prices, payment terms, capacity, lead times, logistics and total cost on one common basis.

  4. 04

    We coordinate the operation

    We handle negotiation, samples, validations, contracting and delivery, with personal attention throughout the process.

Fixed management fee and transparent costs
