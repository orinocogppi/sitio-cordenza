Why it exists

You have the product. Whoever needs it cannot find it.

There are buyers looking right now for what you sell. They do not know you exist, and you do not know they are looking. We put both sides at the same table, with the request in writing and with who answers for what.

How to take part

From presenting your company to getting paid, it is four steps.

These four. No small print and no hidden steps in between.

  1. 01

    Present your company

    You tell us what you manufacture, distribute or represent, where you are and on what terms you serve. That is what makes it possible to match your offer to a specific request.

  2. 02

    Receive applicable opportunities

    When a request comes in that your company can answer, we present it to you with the scope, the quantities, the terms and the validation criteria already organised.

  3. 03

    Send your proposal

    You answer with your specifications, your documentation, your prices, your lead times and your payment terms, in the format you already work in.

  4. 04

    Move towards the sale

    We coordinate negotiation, samples, validations, contracting and delivery, with personal attention throughout the process.

Registration and participation at no cost until the first sale closes

Presenting your company, receiving opportunities and sending proposals carries no cost. The charge appears when an operation closes, not before.

What it means for you

That an offer is not discarded for arriving in another format, another currency or another scope. It is brought to the same basis as the rest and assessed for what it really offers.

What you need to tell us

Six things about you, and what each one is for.

With this the opportunity reaches you and not someone else. None of it is published: we use it to know who to call when an order comes in that you can fulfil.

01

Products

What you manufacture, distribute or which brand you represent, in as much detail as it takes to match it against a specification.

02

Services

What you can do besides selling: installation, commissioning, support, spare parts or whatever your operation includes.

03

Capacity

What volume you can handle and on what terms, so we do not bring you an opportunity you cannot fulfil.

04

Location

Where you ship from. It determines lead times, logistics and total cost, which are three of the nine comparison criteria.

05

Terms of service

Currencies, payment methods, minimums, lead times and delivery bases you normally work with.

06

Confidentiality

We protect identities, requests, budgets, suppliers and contractual terms. Results are communicated with authorisation or anonymised.

Each operation strengthens the next. The commercial data, the capacities on record and the relationships built stay organised so new opportunities can be answered with greater precision.
